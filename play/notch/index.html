The idea.

When Claude Code boots, a little pixel apple greets you. This gives that apple a job: it lives in the MacBook notch and peeks out to tell you when your assistant is thinking, ready, or in trouble.

Currently a design demo. A real version would be a small native app that listens for Claude Code’s Stop hook and animates the notch in place.
